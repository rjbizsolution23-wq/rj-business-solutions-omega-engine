AI Style v1.0

Rule 01 · The anti-generic test.

Every AI-generated asset must pass this test: if the RJ logo disappeared, would this still look like RJ? If no — reject. Here's what that looks like in practice.

DO · Reads as RJ instantly
✓
RJ blue palette (#2563EB → #0EA5E9)

Gradient anchored in these exact hex values. Never off-brand blues.

✓
Space Grotesk headings, Inter body

Specified explicitly. Never system defaults, never Times.

✓
Real human photography

Diverse skin tones · natural lighting · office / desk / meeting context.

✓
Editorial confidence

Clear focal point · negative space · restrained color · one hero element.

✓
Structural geometry

Sharp corners at exact radii (8px · 12px · 16px). Grid-based layouts.

✓
Compliance-aware tone

Copy that says "est." not "guaranteed" · avoids specific score claims.

DON'T · Reads as AI slop
✗
Rainbow gradients

Pink · purple · orange · teal mixed. Screams AI. Screams generic SaaS 2019.

✗
Glowing neon "cyberpunk" edges

Purple/blue neon rims on cards. Fintech-crypto trope. Not us.

✗
Overlapping "3D robot" mascots

No literal robot imagery. No cartoon character mascots. Not fintech.

✗
Isometric 3D floating cards

The "SaaS 2018 illustration" style. Too template. Zero originality.

✗
Stock "diverse team high-fiving"

Cliché stock photography. Feels transactional and inauthentic.

✗
Guaranteed score claims in copy

"+147 points!" · "Boost your score fast!" — compliance nightmare + generic.

Rule 02 · The 4 style pillars.

Every RJ visual carries these four traits. Any prompt should include positive terms from at least three pillars.

Pillar 01 · Structural

Precision · systems · geometry

The visual language of engineered systems. Grid alignment. Clean angles. Sharp corner radii at exact tokens. Charts and data feel first-class.

grid-alignedgeometricstructuralrectilinearsystems-thinkingarchitectural
Pillar 02 · Human

Real people · real contexts

Photography is warm, contemporary, natural-light. Real office and meeting contexts — never studio-lit corporate stock. Skin tones diverse and true-to-life.

natural-lightcandidreal-officediverse-authenticdocumentary-styleKodak-Portra
Pillar 03 · Chromatic

RJ blue · white · restraint

Primary palette locked. Accents from semantic colors only. Never rainbow. Never off-brand hex. Gradients follow the pattern in tokens.json.

RJ-blue#2563EB#0EA5E9deep-navymonochromaticrestrained-palette
Pillar 04 · Editorial

Confidence · calm · earned

Compositions like editorial magazine spreads — not startup landing pages. One focal element. Generous negative space. Type that carries hierarchy.

editorial-compositionnegative-spacetypographic-hierarchyconfidentcalmpremium-craft
Pillar 05 · Compliance-native

Honest math · labeled estimates

All copy involving numbers reads "est." · scenarios are "projected" not "guaranteed" · disclaimers are treated as first-class UI, not fine print.

estimate-labeledconfidence-bandnon-committal-toneFCRA-awareCROA-safe
Pillar 06 · Motion-restrained

Purposeful · never decorative

If motion exists, it explains cause → effect. No parallax for its own sake. No decorative loops. Reduced-motion always respected.

200ms-basestd-easingexplanatoryno-parallaxreduced-motion-safe
