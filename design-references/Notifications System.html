Priority scale · 5 levels

Every notification is assigned one priority. Priority determines channel routing, delivery time (respecting quiet hours), and whether it bypasses do-not-disturb.

01 · Informational
FYI · dismissable
Report refreshed · new article published · monthly summary. In-app only, digest email.
02 · Positive
Good news · celebrate
Score increase · dispute won · milestone reached. In-app + push, digest email.
03 · Action needed
Something to do
Review dispute response · pay before statement close · confirm inquiry. In-app + push + email.
04 · Warning
Time-sensitive
Payment failed · SLA at risk · monitoring gap detected. In-app + push + email + SMS.
05 · Urgent · security
Bypasses DND
Suspicious login · new account attempted · identity alert. All channels · bypasses quiet hours.

Notification center · inbox model

Persistent inbox. Everything routes here regardless of channel. Unread count in bell icon. Filters by category and priority. Bulk mark-read supported.
